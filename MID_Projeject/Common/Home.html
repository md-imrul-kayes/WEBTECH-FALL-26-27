Welcome to FoodExpress

FoodExpress is an online food delivery system where customers can order food from their favorite restaurants, restaurant owners can manage their menu and orders, delivery men can deliver orders quickly, and admins can manage the whole system.

About Us

FoodExpress is a university Web technology course group project that demonstrates a multi-user web system with four user categories: Admin, Customer, Restaurant Owner and Delivery Man.
